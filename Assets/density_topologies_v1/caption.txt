Representative communication topologies sampled from three SUMO traffic-density scenarios: ETSI #1 (35 veh/km), ETSI #3 (123 veh/km), and ETSI #6 (500 veh/km). Each panel shows four V2V transmitter-receiver pairs and four V2I users from the near-BS subset of rep9_k4. Arrows indicate V2V links. Horizontal scales differ; vehicle symbols and lane spacing are schematic. Only selected communication vehicles are shown, rather than all traffic. Base stations, V2I uplink edges, and interference edges are omitted for clarity.
